\section{Related Work}

The final paper should compare Loomstead with four groups of work. First, believable generative-agent simulations such as Generative Agents and Concordia establish the reference point for persistent social simulation, memory, reflection, planning, and grounded agent environments~\cite{park2023generativeAgents,vezhnevets2023concordia}. Second, drama-management work frames the long-standing tension between authorial control and player autonomy in interactive narrative~\cite{roberts2008dramaManagementSurvey}. Third, multi-agent orchestration systems such as AutoGen, MetaGPT, and ChatDev provide task-oriented baselines based on conversation, roles, SOPs, and explicit workflow decomposition~\cite{wu2023autogen,hong2024metagpt,qian2024chatdev}. Finally, AgentBench motivates evaluation in interactive environments, while Loomstead narrows the evaluation target to process-constrained narrative goals and traceable social evidence~\cite{liu2024agentbench}.

The literature review is tracked in \texttt{paper/lit\_review/}. The current citation set is a seed list rather than a final related-work section.
