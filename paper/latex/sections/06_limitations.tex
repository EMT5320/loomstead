\section{Limitations}

The current evidence should be read as a research-preview scaffold. The process suite uses small sample counts, the Hard Delegation baseline remains synthetic, and several ablations are posterior analyses. C16 robustness promotion currently remains \texttt{needs\_manual\_review}, and reviewer-packet manual sampling has not finished. The paper still needs more seeds, LLM-backed runs, human process-believability ratings, and real Godot observer-mode validation.
